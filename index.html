About the project

Meet Optimum

Optimum is a Universal Data Acceleration Network focused on improving how data moves across blockchains. It helps networks propagate critical data faster while using less bandwidth.

Optimum is not a Layer 1 or Layer 2 blockchain. It works alongside existing chains at the data propagation layer.

Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official Optimum website?

No. This Contributor Hub is an independent community project created by dexblocker. It is not operated or endorsed by the Optimum team.

Where do my contribution statistics come from?

The hub uses available Discord membership details and publicly accessible community leaderboard data. Stats can be delayed or unavailable; they do not represent a verified count of all content contributions.

Do I need to join the Optimum Discord community?

The hub is designed for Optimum Discord community members. Available stats depend on your account and whether it appears in the accessible community data.

Are these badges official Optimum rewards?

No. They are visual recognition tiers created for this independent hub, not official Discord roles, tokens, or financial rewards.

Can I share my card?

Yes. Download your card as a PNG and attach it to a post on X. The FLEX ON X action prepares a post; image attachment is manual.
